## Immediate findings

- The verified watchdog succeeded on November 3: [run 41733716800](https://github.com/diegoduque16/Global-economic-policy-tool/actions/runs/41733716800).
- The ILOSTAT collector runs three times daily and has 360/1,964 compressed source files verified. These are source-native archives and not canonical integrated observations.
- Canonical baseline: 2,780,117 verified numeric observations, 24 families; source-native table: 2,104,609 additional records, not harmonized additions.
- Continuous scheduled collection is *not proof* of automatic canonical integration and research-panel freshness.
- The legacy BEA installer generated 93 failures; the BEA verified v3 installer succeeded, but inspect workflow references before disabling anything.
- The same-content `atlas-first-wid-wdi-research 2.yml` and `atlas-first-wid-wdi-research-2.yml` pair is an obvious duplicate review candidate.

## Safest consolidation sequence

1. **Protect** scheduled production collectors, successful canonical snapshots, OneDrive backup paths and their SHA256 evidence.
2. **Map dependencies** from `workflow_run`, `gh run download`, pinned run IDs, and `.github/workflows/` paths before disabling any workflow.
3. **Review/retire duplicates and failed installers** by disabling obsolete jobs first, retaining committed code and old run evidence.
4. **Create one authoritative data-lineage registry**: source → raw version → numeric verification → canonical version → research panel version → backed-up archive.
5. **Pilot end-to-end incremental integration** on World Bank WDI before extending to WID and relevant ILOSTAT series.
6. **Extend the watchdog** to identify stale or disconnected canonical and research outputs, not just successful collection.
